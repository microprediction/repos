Longer write-ups, demos and papers, one topic each. Amber cards are research and educational sites; the purple boxes above are packages.

researchpitllm.microprediction.org

Point-in-time language models: the released checkpoint sequences (ChronoBERT, Time Machine GPT, DatedGPT, TimeLMs, StoriesLM), a verified bibliography of the look-ahead-bias literature with a one-paragraph “key idea” note per paper, a literature map, a timeline, and dated corpora.

models·key ideas·literature map researchwinning.microprediction.org

Races, correlated or not: a generalization of factor multinomial probit models that scales to millions of variables. In-browser demos (the model live, share calibration, the photo-finish circuit), the factor probit paper, and rating systems benchmarked on twelve datasets. The thurstone package is a compatibility shim over this one.

demos·ratings researchschur.microprediction.org

Schur portfolio literature. The unification of hierarchical and optimisation-based portfolio construction via block-matrix inversion, now part of skfolio and allocation.

papers·literature map·timeline researchconformalprediction.net

A small theoretical contribution: a KL-divergence theorem explaining clearly the limitations of conformal prediction, with demos.

papers·demos surveymechanisms.microprediction.org

Survey of markets, scoring rules and everything in between.

papers researchinventory.microprediction.org

Control theory applied to inventory and pricing: notes, preprints and a literature map for storable goods whose carrying cost is nearly zero, where the dealer's quoted width and skew do the work that cost of carry usually does.
